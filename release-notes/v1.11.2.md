## Download

[**RansomGuard.exe**](https://github.com/leo-code-del/RansomGuard/releases/download/v1.11.2/RansomGuard.exe) (Windows 10 / 11)

**Update:** click **Check for updates** under **Tools** in the control panel (before 1.11.0: on the main window; 1.7.3: **Update to the latest version**).

**Verify the download:** with the [GitHub CLI](https://cli.github.com/) installed, run `gh attestation verify RansomGuard.exe --repo leo-code-del/RansomGuard`.

## What's new in 1.11.2

- On the control panel's home page, only the row under the mouse is gray: the first row stayed gray after the mouse moved on to the others
