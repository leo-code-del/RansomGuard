## Download

[**RansomGuard.exe**](https://github.com/leo-code-del/RansomGuard/releases/download/v1.10.0/RansomGuard.exe) (Windows 10 / 11)

**Update:** click **Check for updates** in the control panel (1.7.3: **Update to the latest version**).

**Verify the download:** with the [GitHub CLI](https://cli.github.com/) installed, run `gh attestation verify RansomGuard.exe --repo leo-code-del/RansomGuard`.

## What's new in 1.10.0 — self-check

- New **Self-check** in the control panel makes sure protection really works on this computer: background monitoring and the process monitor are running, in the control panel's version; the canary files are intact and hidden; only administrators can change the state folder; no errors since monitoring started; the network and backup state
- It ends with a drill in a temporary folder: simulated encryption of 12 documents and a changed canary file, in test mode (no network cut, no warning), and shows how fast each was detected
- About 10 seconds; nothing is changed
- The control panel is more compact and fits laptop screens of 768 pixels; on taller screens the progress area shows more lines

## 1.10.0 更新內容 — 自我檢查

**更新方式：** 在控制台按 **檢查版本更新**（1.7.3：**更新到最新版本**）。

**驗證下載的檔案：** 安裝 [GitHub CLI](https://cli.github.com/) 後，執行 `gh attestation verify RansomGuard.exe --repo leo-code-del/RansomGuard`。

- 控制台新增 **自我檢查**，確認防護在這台電腦上真的在運作：背景監控和程序監控有在執行、版本和控制台相同；誘餌檔完好且隱藏；狀態資料夾只有系統管理員能修改；監控這次啟動後沒有錯誤；網路和備份狀態
- 最後在暫存資料夾演練一次：以測試模式模擬加密 12 個文件、修改一個誘餌檔（不會斷網、不會跳出警告），並顯示各花了幾秒被偵測到
- 約 10 秒，不會更改任何東西
- 控制台版面更精簡，768 像素高的筆電螢幕也放得下；螢幕較高時，執行進度區會顯示更多行
