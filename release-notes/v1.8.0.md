## What's new in 1.8.0 — stronger detection

Detection now also catches the ways newer ransomware avoids simple checks:

- **Partial and intermittent encryption:** ransomware that leaves a file's header alone and encrypts the rest, or only blocks spread through it, to be faster. Office files, zip archives and PDFs are checked at their end and where each part starts, once nobody is writing them any more; large text files at their end
- **Encrypted copies:** ransomware that writes an encrypted copy under another name (`photo.jpg` → `a8Fk2.b3c1`) and deletes the original
- **More kinds of files:** camera RAW photos (Canon, Nikon, Sony, Fujifilm, Panasonic, Olympus, Pentax, Samsung, DNG), Outlook mailboxes, KeePass password databases, Access databases, AutoCAD drawings, and more Office, audio and video formats
- **Fewer false alarms:** programs that save through a temporary file and rename it at once (editors, npm and other developer tools: `notes.md.tmp.1724.4af6f2` → `notes.md`) no longer count as files renamed to unknown extensions. In a watched folder with code or notes, a burst of saves could cut the network

Tested with 11 simulated ransomware techniques, all caught within about 1 to 4 seconds, and 11 kinds of everyday work (unpacking projects, Git, Office saves, camera imports, slow network copies, torrent downloads, npm installs...) that don't trigger it. Checking files this way adds no noticeable time.

## 1.8.0 更新內容 — 偵測能力加強

**更新方式：** 在控制台按 **檢查版本更新**（1.7.3：**更新到最新版本**）。

**驗證下載的檔案：** 安裝 [GitHub CLI](https://cli.github.com/) 後，執行 `gh attestation verify RansomGuard.exe --repo leo-code-del/RansomGuard`。

偵測現在也能抓到新型勒索病毒用來躲過簡單檢查的手法：

- **部分加密、間歇加密：** 不動檔頭、只加密後段，或一段一段跳著加密以求更快的勒索病毒。Office 檔、zip 壓縮檔和 PDF 會在沒有程式寫入之後檢查檔案結尾和每個內部檔案的開頭；較大的文字檔會檢查結尾
- **加密副本：** 用別的檔名寫出加密副本（`photo.jpg` → `a8Fk2.b3c1`）、再刪掉原檔的勒索病毒
- **更多檔案類型：** 相機 RAW 照片（Canon、Nikon、Sony、Fujifilm、Panasonic、Olympus、Pentax、Samsung、DNG）、Outlook 郵件檔、KeePass 密碼庫、Access 資料庫、AutoCAD 圖檔，以及更多 Office、音訊和影片格式
- **更少誤判：** 先寫暫存檔、再立刻改名存檔的程式（編輯器、npm 等開發工具：`notes.md.tmp.1724.4af6f2` → `notes.md`）不再被算成「檔案被改成陌生副檔名」。之前在放程式碼或筆記的監控資料夾裡連續存檔，可能會切斷網路

用 11 種模擬的勒索病毒手法測試，全部在約 1 到 4 秒內抓到；11 種日常操作（解壓專案、Git、Office 存檔、相機匯入照片、慢速網路複製、BT 下載、npm 安裝……）都不會誤判。這些檢查不會讓電腦明顯變慢。
